## Interaction verification

- Scenes 0→5 reached by scrolling, in order; scrolling back reverses them (the observer
  writes the index of whichever caption crosses the middle band; nothing is one-way).
- The chart draws once on scene 4 (`chartDraw` on a `pathLength="1"` path); figures arrive at
  300/620/940/1260 ms after it, one at a time (P6).
- Reduced motion: transitions collapse to 0.01ms by the universal rule; `.story-line` and
  `.story-caret` are killed by name and settle drawn / visible. Scenes still switch.
- Print: every `[data-for]` piece is shown static; the stack is unpinned.
- No link or button exists inside the `aria-hidden` stage; the closing band carries both
  CTAs. `tests/lp-variants.spec.ts` asserts this.

## What is NOT verified here

- Real scroll feel on a trackpad vs a wheel (the observer's 20% band was chosen by
  reasoning, not measured with users).
- Safari's `svh` behaviour with the URL bar; only Chromium was captured.
- Whether a first-time visitor can say what the product does at scene 5 — that is the
  review's question (`/lp`).
